IThe Shire

Every journey begins somewhere ordinary. Mine began with a computer lab and a love of reading.

I am Arman Shaikh R., a Computer Science teacher at Swamy Vivekananda Rural First Grade College (SVRES), Niveditha Campus, Neraluru, Anekal Taluk. I teach BCA students, look after laboratory work, and help run the IGNOU Study Centre as Assistant Coordinator.

I like explaining hard things in simple words, and I like students who can explain their own work. Outside the classroom I read, write, and think about how technology and society shape each other.
